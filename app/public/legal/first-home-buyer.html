First Home Buyer Guide

This short guide highlights practical steps for first home buyers in Australia. It is an overview only — check your state government pages for grants and stamp duty concessions.

1. Budget and borrowing power

Estimate how much you can borrow using the calculators on this site. Include deposit, stamp duty, fees, and a buffer for rate changes.

2. Grants & concessions

Many states offer first home buyer grants or stamp duty reductions. Search your state government website for up-to-date eligibility and application steps.

3. Loan types & interest rates

Compare fixed vs variable rates, loan features and fees. Consider speaking to a mortgage broker for tailored options.

4. Inspecting properties

  • Check location, transport, schools and future development.
  • Factor in maintenance and renovation costs.

5. Contracts & legal

Have a conveyancer or solicitor review contracts before exchanging. Understand cooling-off periods and settlement timelines.

Useful links

  • State government housing pages (search your state)
  • Reserve Bank of Australia (RBA) — for official interest rate information

For personalised advice, consult a qualified mortgage broker or financial adviser.
