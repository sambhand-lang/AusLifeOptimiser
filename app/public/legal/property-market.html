Property Market Overview

This page provides a brief overview of factors that drive property markets and guidance on using data responsibly.

Key market drivers

  • Supply & demand: new housing supply, population growth, and migration.
  • Interest rates: mortgage interest rates influence affordability and demand.
  • Local amenity: transport, schools and employment nodes affect local prices.
  • Policy: tax settings, grants, and zoning rules can alter market dynamics.

Using data

Our suburb metrics and comparisons pull together public datasets and derived indicators. Use them as one input among many — combine with on-the-ground inspection and professional advice.

Sources

Where possible rely on official sources such as the Australian Bureau of Statistics and the RBA, and consult property market reports from reputable research firms for deeper analysis.
