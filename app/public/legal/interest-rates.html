Interest Rates

This page gives a high-level explanation about interest rates and where to check current official rates.

Official cash rate

The Reserve Bank of Australia (RBA) sets the official cash rate which influences bank lending rates. For the latest official rate, visit the RBA website: rba.gov.au.

Home loan rates

Major banks and lenders publish variable and fixed mortgage rates. Market rates can differ from the official cash rate due to competition, risk pricing and fees.

How to use rates on this site

Our calculators use an indicative rate for modelling. Always check current lender offers and, where appropriate, consult a broker for personalised comparisons.
